The Evidence Column

A score is the top of a column, not the whole of it.

Scroll to section the column. Every layer beneath the number is something that actually happened — and every one of them is visible to you.

01 / 06 — THE SCORE

Most tools stop here.

A number, and a shrug. You learn that you scored 80. You do not learn what an 80 is made of, so you cannot do anything differently tomorrow.

02 / 06 — THE CRITERIA

Every score is a weighted sum.

Four criteria, fixed weights, applied identically to everyone: correctness ·45, delivery ·25, clarity ·15, structure ·15. The weights are published, not inferred.

03 / 06 — THE EVIDENCE

Each criterion points at words.

Not a paraphrase of your answer — the exact span that earned or cost you points, quoted back with the reason attached.

04 / 06 — THE SIGNALS

Underneath: what you actually did.

What your voice did, what your code did, and what you left unsaid. Pace, filler density, pause length, test results, static analysis findings.

05 / 06 — THE HISTORY

One column joins the others.

Semantic deduplication across sessions means the same weakness twice is a pattern, not a coincidence. "Vague about scale" in a design round and "no metrics" in a behavioural round resolve to one tracked gap.

06 / 06 — THE STACK

That's the whole column.

Nothing hidden, nothing inferred, nothing you cannot trace back to something you said, wrote or ran.

The Difference

A tool that only flatters you is a tool that cannot help you.

This is what an evaluation actually looks like when nothing is softened. It is unflattering on purpose — that is the entire value.

Act V · Before you start

The questions worth answering directly.

Is this cheating?

Practice Mode and Mock Interview are preparation tools — you are answering questions and being scored on them, which is the opposite of being fed answers. The AI Copilot is a study aid for working through problems you do not yet understand. Using any real-time assistant during a live interview you have been asked to complete unaided is not something we will help you do, and it is not what the scoring engine is built for. The product's whole value is telling you what you actually cannot do yet.

How is a score calculated, exactly?

Four weighted criteria — correctness ·45, delivery ·25, clarity ·15, structure ·15 — summed and normalised to 100. If a dimension had no input, it is excluded and the remaining weights are renormalised. A typed coding answer has no delivery signal, so delivery is not scored zero; it is not scored at all. Zeroing an unmeasured dimension is the single most common way scoring engines lie to you.

Do I need to turn my camera on?

No. Proctoring is opt-in per session. With it off, the session is scored identically on everything that does not require video. With it on, detection runs on-device and only the signal — not the frames — is transmitted.

Which companies are covered?

Fifteen profiles, including Google, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Apple, Netflix, Airbnb and Uber. Questions are generated against retrieved context for the company and role rather than pulled from a static bank, which is why they do not go stale the way a bank does.

What does the free tier actually include?

The modules, the scoring engine and the explanations — not a watered-down preview of them. Bring your own provider key and the economics work, which is why there is no card field in the way. See the documentation for current limits.

Can I export a session?

Yes — Mock Interview produces an exportable HTML report with the full question set, your answers, the per-criterion breakdown and the evidence spans behind each score.
